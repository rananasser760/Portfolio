Quantum Computing

Coursework in quantum algorithms

Alongside AI, I studied quantum computing with Qiskit and Pennylane, working through the algorithms that define the field's early speedups over classical computation:

Deutsch

The first proof that a quantum circuit can answer a question in fewer queries than any classical algorithm.

Deutsch–Jozsa

Generalizes Deutsch's idea to decide constant vs. balanced functions in a single query.

Simon's

Finds a hidden bitwise period exponentially faster than any classical approach — a direct precursor to Shor's.

Grover's

Searches an unsorted space of N items in roughly √N steps via amplitude amplification.

Shor's

Factors large integers in polynomial time — the algorithm behind quantum computing's threat to RSA encryption.
